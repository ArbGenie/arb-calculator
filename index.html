Why round at all? Bookmakers watch for stakes calculated to the kobo. Ordinary punters do not bet ₦4,040.63 — they bet ₦4,000. Staking exact odd figures over and over is one of the quickest ways to be flagged as an arber and limited.

But check after you round. Round too far and a guaranteed profit becomes a guaranteed loss. Edit the stakes above and this page tells you straight away whether the profit survived.
⚠️ Always check both odds on the bookmaker's own screen before staking. Odds move in seconds.

ArbGenie Arb Calculator — a stake-splitting tool for sports arbitrage, built for members of the ArbGenie Telegram channel.

Everything runs in your own browser. There is no server, no account and no login. The page asks for no personal information, stores nothing, and transmits nothing. The only external file it loads is Telegram’s own telegram-web-app.js, which lets it match your Telegram theme.
